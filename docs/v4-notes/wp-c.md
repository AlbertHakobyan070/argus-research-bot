### Decisions where the spec is silent

- **Dedupe is cross-section only** (as `cross_section_duplicate_ratio` measures it). A repeat inside one section is the writer's style, and its sentence often backs another finding.
- **The merge neighbour is the nearest surviving section** (previous first). Since fix round 1 the outline is rewritten (the merge sticks) — also when the neighbour's merged re-write is skipped: the neighbour then owns the merged sub-questions and notes, and its next re-write in the round writes them.
- **The flat writer's notes are every usable note** (`note_ids=None`): flat lengths have no outline to allocate; it has the sections' 24 000-char budget.
- **Extraction ids**: the sentence's markers that the model's `source_ids` name; when the model names none of them, every marker of the sentence (the sentence is what the reader sees and what the cut locates — never a finding without ids for a sentence that cites).
- **The rewrite ratio's denominator is `judged`**, so a half-skipped judge cannot dilute a real flag rate.
- **A judge reply without a `flagged` list is unparseable** — never "nothing flagged" (the v3 failure mode).
- **The diversity rerun reruns every batch** and counts only when it judged at least as many findings; its events are not re-emitted.
- **report_builder recomputes the uncited specifics over the cut body** (the reader's text); the panel's `uncited_specifics` is the pre-cut metric.
- **TL;DR**: with an analysis, only its takeaways count (no fallback to section sentences — "nothing kept → omitted"), in the analyst's order, each still contained (normalised) in the cut analysis; without one, the first kept finding of each section whose sentence is contained (normalised) in the cut section — never a fuzzy match (R2 at `46191fa`). A run without sections (no usable evidence) has no TL;DR and no omission line.
- **`degraded`** = the degraded-run banner, any unjudged finding or any unjudged section (a section or the Analysis citing `[n]` with no finding). Grounding switched off shows "⚠ Not fact-checked — the grounding judge is off" but is a configuration, not a degradation; with it off no section is called unjudged.
- **The degraded-run banner** counts per per-item role (`research.digest`, `compose.section`, `compose.findings_extract` over the whole run; `panel.grounding` over its last panel pass — the panel verdict's skipped batches of `judge_verdicts["grounding_batches"]`): items = its ok records (diversity reruns excluded) plus its `skip:` lines; the reason is "unparseable", else the 429/5xx code, else the error type. The coverage review is a single-call role: its skip is a degradation note only.
- **The stricter verdict** (R1 at `46191fa`) applies to the findings the verdict cuts — flagged or citing unknown ids only — one level deep, before locating; it uses the locator's normalisation (`quarantine` cannot import compose's `_norm`) at the dedupe threshold, 0.85. A kept twin is located and cut like any other (its own row, reason `near-duplicate of cut f<n>`).
- **A finding stated in a cut span** is one whose located sentences all lie inside the section's cut sentences; its reason joins the row of the span that cut its first sentence.
- **An unknown id beside known ones** is judged per sentence: a kept sentence citing a known id loses its unknown ones; a sentence citing unknown ids only stays (the unknown-id rule cuts it when a finding states it).
- **Degradation notes** show the bench's `skip:` / `degrade:` / `legacy:` lines plus `pdf:`, `tldr:`, `cancelled:` and `compose:` (a merge that lost notes) — the visible losses. `analysis:` lines (pointer 20) are informational: they stay in `errors` and evidence.json `errors`, not in the notes, `warnings` or `degradations`. evidence.json `degradations` and metadata.json `degradations` are exactly the bench set.
- **The citation-integrity passes run over the report up to Sources**, not over the degradation notes, the footer or the lecture appendix: a note naming a source that could not be digested keeps its URL.
- **The title block's `findings:` count is the kept findings.**
- **WP-0 decision 6 is superseded**: the flat writer no longer emits headings of its own, so the flat path is on `assemble_draft` (`## Findings`). (Its stated reason was also off for `tldr`: `TLDR_SYSTEM` always wrote a bare paragraph; only `FLAT_SYSTEM` — `short` and one-sub-question briefs — emitted `## TL;DR` / `## Key Findings`.)

### Behaviour changes for handoff.md

- Flagged and unknown-id sentences are removed from the report (whole sentences), listed in "## ⚠ Flagged claims (removed from the body)"; a flag that cannot be located stays and is listed under "## ⚠ Flagged but not located (still in body)". The v3 report kept them in the body under an appendix that said they were removed.
- The TL;DR is compiled from the kept takeaways (or the first kept finding per section), labelled; no LLM TL;DR. Lecture reports keep "Executive TL;DR".
- The analyst runs between compose and the panel, so medium and longer reports (and short, unless `compose.analyst` is off) get the "## Analysis" section.
- The panel can send the run back to research for a gap wave; it rewrites only for a real flag rate or a weak section, at most `panel.max_compose_revisions` times per round.
- New report surface: the engines / recency / research lines, the "⚠ Not fact-checked" and "⚠ Degraded run" banners, "## Uncited specifics", "## Degradation notes", Sources over the appendices, the flat body under "## Findings".

A run with no library row is a warning in the log; a library exception on that write surfaces as "⚠️ resume failed: …" (it is not swallowed like the status write), the preview already sent.
- Fix round 1: a section (or the Analysis) that cites sources but has no findings is named in "⚠ Not fact-checked" and marks the run degraded; a single skipped coverage review is a degradation note, no longer a "Degraded run"; a claim the judge cut no longer survives through a near-duplicate (the analysis restating it, the TL;DR printing it); a sentence stated twice is cut twice; the judge's ids are read in any common shape; citation markers in code, links and URLs are left alone.
